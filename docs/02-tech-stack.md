## Tech Stack :

### Frontend:

- A frontend where an authenticated official can upload a document and the live image of the document's owner (using a webcam).

- Then display the details of the individual and risk score of the processed document.

### Database:

To store the data for each processed document

- **Context:** Timestamp, Checkpoint Location, and the specific Officer ID logged into the system.

- **Inputs:** Secure links (bucket URLs) to the uploaded passport image and the live webcam capture.

- **System Output:** The exact OCR data extracted, the face match percentage and any specific tampering flags triggered.

- **The Decision:** The final Risk Score generated by the AI and the manual action taken by the officer (e.g., "Cleared," "Detained," "Sent to Secondary Inspection").
